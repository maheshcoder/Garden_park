Garden controls

Walk with WASD or arrow keys. Drag to turn the camera; scroll to zoom. E interacts with the nearest highlighted object. Select Water to care for flowers and refill at the pond. Select Feed to place seeds, then step back for the birds. Picking flowers replenishes bird food.

Space: plant · B: butterfly · R: rain · N: day/night · P: pause. On touch screens, use the left stick and the action button.
